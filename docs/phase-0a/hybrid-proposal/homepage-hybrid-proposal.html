Contact us
Do I need an account?

No account is needed to browse the Directory. An account is needed to save items and to use AutoFlow AI.

Is AIKitFinder free?

Browsing the public AI Tools Directory is free and does not require an account. Some features may require sign-in. AI tool providers set their own prices. AutoFlow AI pricing has not yet been announced.

How is tool information verified?

Each fact is checked against the tool's official source and dated. Anything not yet checked is labelled. Records marked “Sample” are fictional.

Which AI providers does AutoFlow AI use?

AutoFlow AI is being built to work with several AI providers, starting with Claude. In the first release the provider accounts are managed by AIKitFinder; you do not need your own API keys. You connect your own social accounts when you publish.

Will AutoFlow AI post to my channels automatically?

No. Nothing is published until you review and approve it.
